# Member 4: Localized agricultural chatbot (backend)

## What it does
"Ask SoilMind" answers paddy farmers' questions in **English and Sinhala** (and Singlish). It uses Department of
Agriculture documents (RRDI, NAICC) and, for "my field" questions, the plot's live data from Members 2 and 3.

**The rule for this component: no outside AI service.** No Gemini, OpenAI, Claude or similar API. Every model is
trained or fine-tuned by us on our own data and runs locally (open pretrained starting points are allowed).

```
question -> language detect -> [1] intent classifier (ours, fine-tuned XLM-R)
                              -> [2] retriever (ours, fine-tuned MiniLM; + BM25 for English) over DOA/RRDI passages
                              -> [3] live field data (Member 2/3 APIs) for "my field" questions
                              -> [4] extractive answer: the best 1-2 sentences (or the table) are copied, with citations
                                     + bilingual templates filled from the APIs (exact amounts and dates)
                              -> [5] guardrail: refuse + escalate when out of scope or the retriever score is too low
```

**Status: Phase 6 done (change of plan): extractive RAG, no text generator.** A number in an answer can only
come from a cited DOA/RRDI passage or from Member 2/3's APIs.

**Data honesty.** All training and test questions in `data/training/` are generated by code, drafted by the AI
coding assistant, or machine-translated, and **none is human-checked** (see `data/training/DATASET.md`). The only
file the team reviews is the 60-question evaluation set `eval/qa_testset.xlsx` (see `eval/README.md`).

| Route | What |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/m4/chat` | `{session_id, message, persona, ui_lang}` -> answer, path, intent, citations, retrieved passages, live values, escalation |
| `GET /api/m4/sources` | knowledge-base documents, extraction method, passages, how many wait for OCR |
| `GET /api/m4/escalations` | refused questions forwarded to an agriculture officer (MongoDB `escalations`) |

## How it works (Phase 5)

### 1. Documents → passages
| Step | Command | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Download | `python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.download` | Fetches the documents listed in `member4_chatbot/collect/sources.yaml` into `data/docs/raw/` (RRDI fertilizer pages, a bounded crawl of RRDI's disease / pest / weed / crop-establishment / variety / technology pages in English and Sinhala, RRDI books and leaflets, NAICC leaflets and crop calendars from Google Drive). Writes `manifest.yaml` (title, URL, language, publisher, date retrieved, status). |
| Ingest | `python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.ingest` | Extracts text, checks Sinhala, cleans, chunks → `data/processed/passages.csv` (id, text, lang, doc, page, url, …) and `extraction_log.csv` |

**Extraction method per document** (`ingest/extract.py`), logged in `extraction_log.csv`:
1. `manual_text`: a person-supplied text in `data/docs/manual_text/` always wins.
2. `html_text`: web pages (already Unicode).
3. `text_layer`: the PDF's own text, if it reads as real Sinhala / English.
4. `legacy_font_map`: FM-style legacy font mapped to Unicode, if the result reads as Sinhala.
5. `ocr_tesseract`: Tesseract `sin+eng` at 300 dpi.
6. `skipped_needs_ocr`: none of the above worked (Tesseract not installed).

**The Sinhala check** (`ingest/sinhala.py`) uses three numbers: the share of letters in the Sinhala block
U+0D80–U+0DFF; the share of very common Sinhala words; and the share of vowel signs that do not follow a
consonant. Real Sinhala has about 0.2 common-word rate and under 1% broken signs. Scanner garbage mapped
through a legacy-font table has 15–70% broken signs, so it is rejected. On these documents, every "Sinhala"
PDF turned out to be a **scan with a Latin OCR layer added by the scanner**, not a legacy font. These scans need
Tesseract.

**Cleaning** (`ingest/clean.py`):
- NFC normalisation; ZWJ (U+200D) kept, because Sinhala needs it (ශ්‍රී, ප්‍රමාණය); other invisible characters
  removed.
- Running headers and footers are removed: a short line among the first and last two lines of a page that repeats
  on at least half the pages.
- Web-site furniture is removed: lines on at least a quarter of all saved pages.
- Page numbers and Tamil language links are removed.

**Chunking** (`ingest/chunk.py`):
- About 150–300 words per passage.
- A heading starts a passage and stays at its top.
- Tables are never split.
- Long paragraphs are split at sentence ends.
- Repeated sections (tabbed web pages) are kept once.
- A short tail is merged into the passage before it.

**Language detection** (`lang.py`): counts Sinhala vs Latin letters. A small list of common romanised words
recognises Singlish (`si_latn`).

### 2. Training dataset
`python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.build_dataset` → `data/training/intents.csv`, `qa_pairs.csv`,
`out_of_scope.csv`, with a `split` column. **Read `data/training/DATASET.md`**: it explains exactly how every row
was made (templates from RRDI tables, hand-drafted questions, rule-based paraphrases, NLLB-200 translation run
locally), how big each part is, and what still needs a human check.

### 3. Model 1: intent classifier (`notebooks/01_intent_classifier.ipynb`)
`xlm-roberta-base` is fine-tuned to sort a question into one of 12 intents. A question whose top probability is
below the threshold τ (chosen on validation) is treated as out of scope. It is compared with TF-IDF + logistic
regression. Saved to `artifacts/intent/`.

### 4. Model 2: retriever (`notebooks/02_retriever.ipynb`)
`paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2` is fine-tuned on question → passage pairs with
MultipleNegativesRankingLoss. It is compared with BM25 and with the pretrained model on test passages it never
saw. All passages are indexed in ChromaDB (`data/chroma/`). Saved to `artifacts/retriever/`.

### 5. Answer step (`member4_chatbot/chat/`, `notebooks/03_extractive_answers.ipynb`)
- `pipeline.py`: language, follow-ups (last 3 turns), intent, refusal, live templates, retrieval, extraction,
  citations.
- `retrieval.py`: dense (ours) or dense + BM25 rank fusion; chosen per language on validation (English RRF,
  Sinhala dense).
- `extractive.py`: sentence / table-row scoring with our retriever model, plus the number safety check.
- `text.py`: every fixed bilingual text.
- `live.py`: Member 2/3 over in-process HTTP.
- `store.py`: sessions and escalations.

Notebook 03 chooses on validation the ranking mode, the refusal threshold τ_r (0.32) and the sentence settings (2
sentences, margin 0.12). It saves `artifacts/answer/config.json`, which the API loads.

### 5b. Sentence reranker and field context (`notebooks/05_sentence_reranker.ipynb`, `chat/rerank.py`, `chat/profile.py`)
- **Our sentence reranker** (a fine-tuned multilingual MiniLM cross-encoder) picks the 1-3 sentences / table rows
  of the best passage. It was trained on auto-generated labels: a sentence is relevant if it contains a key fact of
  the expected answer. The labels are not human-checked.
- **Field context:** fertilizer, liming and "my field" questions prefer RRDI pages matching the plot's zone, water
  regime and age class (read from Member 2's API); the question's own words win.
- Settings were chosen on validation; the 60-question set was not used.

### 6. End-to-end evaluation (`eval/`, `notebooks/04_end_to_end_evaluation.ipynb`)
`python -m member4_chatbot.eval.import_testset` (spreadsheet -> CSV) and `python -m member4_chatbot.eval.run_eval`
(every question through `POST /api/m4/chat`). The results are labelled **UNCHECKED** until the team has reviewed
the spreadsheet.

### 7. Sinhala voice (Phase 6b: `member4_chatbot/voice/`, notebooks 06 and 07)
Speech in and out, all local, all open pretrained models used **as they are** (our own STT fine-tuning waits for
the team's recordings; everything for it is ready):

| Module | What |
|---|---|
| `audio.py` | browser audio (webm / ogg / wav) -> ffmpeg -> 16 kHz mono, silence trimmed at both ends (-40 dB), capped at 30 s. Uses `ffmpeg` on PATH, else the static build from `imageio-ffmpeg` |
| `stt.py` | Whisper fine-tuned for Sinhala via `transformers`; `STT_MODEL` = `small` (`seniruk/whisper-small-si`, CPU default), `small-sinhaspeech` (`SinhaSpeech/whisper-small-sinhala-run11`), `large` (`kasunw/whisper-large-v3-sinhala`; tested and not recommended: less accurate and slower), `finetuned` (ours, `artifacts/stt/finetuned/`) or any HF id. Greedy decoding, language forced |
| `corrections.py` | the farming-term dictionary `data/voice/stt_corrections.yaml` (misheard word -> right term), whole words only; every replacement logged to `data/voice/logs/stt_corrections.jsonl` and returned to the client |
| `sinhala_numbers.py` | numbers, decimals, fractions (3 1/2 -> තුනහමාර), ranges, %, >/<, dates and times as Sinhala words (final vs combining forms: පහ / පන්සියය) |
| `tts_text.py` | text for the voice: strips URLs / markdown / citations, expands dotted Sinhala abbreviations (කි.ග්‍රෑ.), splits sentences and table rows, picks the language per sentence, reads codes (A3-06, ESC-…, Bg 300), units with their number (`data/voice/tts_terms.yaml`), terms (urea, TSP, MOP, dolomite, pH, N/P/K) and numbers; reports leftovers the voice cannot read |
| `tts.py` | Sinhala: **SinhalaVITS** (`dialoglk/SinhalaVITS-TTS-M2` by default, `TTS_VOICE`; the most intelligible of the 4 voices in notebook 07; MPL-2.0; Coqui VITS on romanized Sinhala, `romanizer.py` vendored from the model repo), fallback **Piper** (`chan4lk/piper-tts-sinhala`, MIT). English: `facebook/mms-tts-eng`. Sentence by sentence, joined with 0.25 s pauses, cached by text hash in `data/voice/tts_cache/` |
| `scoring.py` | WER / CER with the same normalisation on both sides (digits as words, ZWJ removed, abbreviations joined) |
| `evaluate.py` | STT runs over the test sets, dictionary candidates, synthetic test set, TTS evaluation, listening sheet |

Two substitutions against the brief, because the named models do not exist publicly:
`seniruk/whisper-medium-si-v2` (not on Hugging Face; its author's public model `seniruk/whisper-small-si` is used,
plus Whisper large-v3 Sinhala, which tested worse and slower) and `facebook/mms-tts-sin` (MMS-TTS has no Sinhala voice; Piper's Sinhala
voice is the fallback). Also: coqui-tts insists on importing `torchaudio`/`torchcodec`, which have no build for our
torch 2.14 and are unused for VITS inference; `tts.py` registers placeholder modules that raise if touched.

**Recording kit for our fine-tuning** (`eval/voice/`, see its README): `record_prompts.csv` (249 Sinhala farming
phrases, 70/30 finetune/test split by phrase), `recorder.html` (offline recording page, ZIP batches),
`pipeline.voice_recordings` (import), notebook 06 (fine-tune the small model on a Colab T4 with an OpenSLR-52
sample mixed in; WER/CER before vs after on held-out phrases).

## How to run and test
```bash
cd backend/member4-chatbot
python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.download          # ~10 min (books are large); skips files already present
python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.ingest            # ~1 min
python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.build_dataset     # seconds (NLLB only runs for new English text)
python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.run_notebooks     # official training + evaluation: notebooks 00-04 (CPU ~90 min)
python -m member4_chatbot.eval.import_testset        # after the team edits eval/qa_testset.xlsx
python -m member4_chatbot.eval.run_eval              # end-to-end evaluation (~1 min; needs MongoDB, fresh readings)
python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.voice_data        # Phase 6b: OpenSLR-30 test sample (700 MB download)
python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.voice_prompts     # recording script eval/voice/record_prompts.csv
python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.voice_synth       # synthetic farming test set (~15 min CPU)
python -m member4_chatbot.pipeline.voice_eval stt small openslr30   # and: synthetic, --device cuda, tts, listening
cd .. && pytest member4-chatbot/tests                # unit + pipeline + API tests (fast; notebook runs skipped)
pytest member4-chatbot/tests -m slow                 # run all six notebooks without retraining (~15 min)
```
The chat runs inside the main API: `cd backend && uvicorn main:app --port 8000` (the models load at start, ~20 s),
then open `http://localhost:5173/chat`.
To read the scanned leaflets: install Tesseract (e.g. the UB Mannheim Windows build) with the **Sinhala**
language data (`sin.traineddata`). Set `TESSERACT_CMD` if it is not on `PATH`, then re-run ingest, build_dataset
and run_notebooks.

## Phase 7: cross-links, map links, warm speech, graceful degradation

Still no outside AI service: every step below uses our own models and the live APIs.

- **Pre-filled questions** (`POST /prefill`, `chat/text.py` `prefill()`):
  - The twin's "Ask SoilMind" and the Advisory page's "Why?" get their question from here, plus a bilingual label.
  - Examples: "Why is nitrogen low in the south-east part of my field?" / "මගේ කුඹුරේ ගිනිකොන කොටසේ තත්ත්වය කුමක්ද?";
    "Why should I apply dolomite on my field now?".
  - The wording was chosen so that our intent model alone sends it to the live field answer. Tested: 90 of 90
    zone × layer × language questions. The page's own example, "Why is the south-east corner red?", was refused
    (classed `deficiency_symptom`), which is why the wording is not free text.
- **Context** (`ChatRequest.context`, `chat/pipeline.py`):
  - **A zone** gets a zone answer:
    1. that zone's alerts, with values and Member 2's deficiency level
    2. the actions for its critical problems (urea for N, lime for pH)
    3. the whole-field summary
  - **A recommendation** gets an explanation of exactly that action: what and when, Member 2's reason, where,
    the expected effect, and its RRDI / DOA sources as citations.
  - A field question that names a zone ("south-east part", "ගිනිකොන") or a sensor ("A3-06") gets the zone answer
    too, without context.
- **`map_focus`:** the zones and sensors an answer talks about. The chat shows "Show on the map" links, which open
  the twin with them highlighted.
- **Graceful degradation (`degraded`):**
  - If the sentence reranker fails, our retriever's own sentence scores pick the sentences of the best passage,
    with a note.
  - If the intent model fails, the question goes to retrieval, which keeps its refusal threshold.
  - If TTS fails, the page says so and the text answer stays.
- **Speech warm-up** (`voice/warmup.py`):
  - At start, a background thread loads Whisper-small-si, SinhalaVITS and MMS-English, then runs each once: speaks
    a fixed Sinhala question and an English sentence, and transcribes the Sinhala audio.
  - The API is usable meanwhile; startup dropped from about 100 s to about 25 s. `/api/health` shows
    `speech_models`: loading → warming → ready.
  - Measured: load 88 s (mostly reading the Whisper weights), warm-up inferences 2.5 s + 1.5 s + 6.6 s.
- **Speaking part by part** (`POST /tts/parts`, `voice/tts_text.py` `speak_parts()`):
  - The browser asks for part 1 (one sentence), plays it, and fetches the next part meanwhile.
  - For farmers, part 1 is fetched as soon as the answer arrives.
  - Effect: a long Sinhala field answer (about 90 s of speech) starts after about 3–5 s instead of after 25–40 s.
- **Shorter live-data cache:** KPIs and alerts 15 s, advice 30 s. Members 2 and 3 cache per readings version, so
  the chat sees the demo scenario within seconds.
- **Health checks:** chat models present and loaded, Chroma index (546 passages), speech models warm.
- **Tests:**
  - `tests/test_m4_crosslinks.py` (25), real intent model, retriever and reranker:
    - every pre-filled question
    - zone and recommendation answers in both languages
    - map links
    - both degradations
    - the route
  - `tests/test_m4_warmup.py`: warm-up states and the part splitter
